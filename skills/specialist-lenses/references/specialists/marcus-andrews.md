# Marcus Andrews

**Lens:** A product's narrative — the story of why it exists and why the world needs it now — is a strategic asset that shapes every downstream launch, positioning, and campaign decision, not a creative-writing exercise applied after the strategy is already set.

## Public framework

Andrews (Product Marketing Group Lead at HubSpot) practices and teaches **narrative design**: a strategic-narrative-development discipline distinct from a single launch's messaging. His most-cited example is HubSpot's own "Flywheel" narrative, which he helped develop as the successor story to "inbound marketing" — both framed not as feature announcements but as a new way of understanding how the business (and the customer's business) actually works, which is what let HubSpot scale a $10B brand narrative across years and product lines rather than one campaign at a time. His public talks and writing describe a narrative design process (developed independently and drawing on other narrative-focused marketers like Andy Raskin and Drift's Dave Gerhardt) for building that kind of durable story.

## Retail application

This is close kin to this repo's existing `storytelling` skill (customer-as-hero/brand-as-guide narrative construction) but adds the multi-year, multi-launch durability angle specifically: a retail brand's "why now" story (why this product category matters today, not just what the product does) should be able to carry a launch, then the next season's campaign, then a loyalty program's framing, without being reinvented each time. For retail, this means checking a major launch or repositioning against "does this fit the larger story we're already telling, or does it fragment it" before finalizing campaign messaging.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Ensuring a product launch or seasonal campaign reinforces a larger brand narrative instead of introducing a disconnected one-off story; auditing whether years of campaigns actually compound into one coherent narrative or read as unrelated efforts.
**Blind spots:** Developed at a B2B SaaS company with a long sales cycle and content-heavy buyer journey — the *narrative durability* principle generalizes to retail, but HubSpot's specific mechanics (inbound methodology, flywheel diagram) are B2B-software-specific and shouldn't be copied literally.
