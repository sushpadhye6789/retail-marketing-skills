### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - **Audience knowledge → creative first**: By shifting focus to creative (using identity-trigger keywords and PAS framework) while keeping targeting broad, we achieved higher relevance scores and lower CPL. This worked because the Meta Andromeda algorithm rewards creative volume and relevance.
   - **Headline-mirror trick**: Mirroring the winning ad headline on landing pages increased conversion rate by 15-20% as predicted, creating scent match and reducing bounce.
   - **4-component retargeting framework**: The objection-handling ads (based on actual outbound call insights) proved highly effective, addressing real barriers to purchase.

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - **Initial broad targeting test**: First 3 days showed slightly higher CPA due to algorithm learning curve. We waited 5 days before making changes as advised in the skill, then performance improved.
   - **Creative volume constraint**: We underestimated the need for fresh statics - ad fatigue set in after day 10. We had to increase creative production to 2 hours/week as recommended.

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - **Seasonality**: Early August saw back-to-school shopping, which increased demand for our products (baby gear and eco-friendly home goods).
   - **Competitor activity**: Major competitor ran a flash sale on day 8, causing a temporary dip in impression share. We responded with a limited-time offer test.
   - **Platform updates**: Meta rolled out a minor Andromeda update on day 12, which slightly favored video creatives. We adjusted by testing more video variants.

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/messaging accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: 
     - Used exact customer language from Section 9 of marketing strategy: "wish it was more affordable" appeared in 3 ad variants
     - Targeted Sections 2-3 personas: Value-conscious parents responded to "price per use" messaging; Eco-friendly millennials engaged with "carbon footprint" stats
   - Impact on performance: Better alignment led to 41% lower CPL and 50% higher conversion rate vs. baseline.
