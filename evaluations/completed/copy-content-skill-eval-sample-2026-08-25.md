### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Persona-specific copy using PAS framework improved conversions - worked because it addressed specific pains and desires from Sections 3.2 and 3.4
   - Content strategy based on persona information needs increased engagement - worked because it answered real questions target audiences were asking
   - Using customer language verbatim from research increased authenticity - worked because it matched how personas actually talk about their problems
   - Content pillars aligned with persona priorities improved SEO performance - worked because it created comprehensive coverage of topics that mattered to target audiences

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to use generic, benefit-focused copy underperformed - didn't work because it didn't address specific persona motivations
   - Corrected by using the exact language and pain points from marketing strategy Sections 3.2 and 3.4

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major content platform algorithm changes during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Increased demand for "sustainable baby care tips" and "eco-friendly parenting advice" by ~40%
   - Used customer service call transcripts enhanced with persona-tagged queries from marketing strategy

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/messaging accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Copy included phrases like "As an eco-conscious parent, you worry about..." and "Grandparents want to feel confident that..." directly from persona language guidelines; content addressed specific questions from Sections 3.2 and 3.4
