## Style Rules Learned
- Hero headlines: 3-7 words, benefit-first
- Body copy: Under 100 words, scannable
- CTA buttons: 2-4 action words, urgency-focused
- Subject lines: < 50 chars, avoid spam triggers
- Preview text: < 90 chars, complements subject line

## Execution Patterns
- Most successful campaigns start with audience research
- Price sensitivity highest in Q1 and Q3
- Trade customers respond better to margin details than end-consumer benefits
