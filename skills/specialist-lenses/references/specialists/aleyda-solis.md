# Aleyda Solis

**Lens:** AI crawlers behave differently from traditional search crawlers, hitting technical roadblocks most SEO teams never anticipated — the technical foundations (crawlability, structured entity data, international/multilingual architecture) that used to just support rankings now directly gate AI-search visibility too.

## Public framework

Solis (founder of Orainti, 15+ years in international/technical SEO) has repositioned her long-standing technical-SEO practice around AI search specifically: she teaches that crawlability shapes everything downstream in AI search, and that AI-crawler behavior differs meaningfully from classic search-engine crawling in ways most technical audits don't yet check for. Her public framing (webinars, BrightonSEO talks, the "AI Search Action Checklist") treats SEO now as understanding *where* an audience searches and *how each platform* (Google, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) surfaces information, then adapting technical and content architecture to match each environment's specific mechanics rather than a single generic technical-SEO checklist.

## Retail application

This extends `seo-audit` (technical foundations) and `ai-seo` directly: a multi-market or multi-language retailer has technical-SEO complexity (hreflang, market-specific content, crawl budget across a large catalog) that most AI-search advice glosses over — Solis's specific expertise is exactly this intersection of international technical SEO and the newer AI-crawlability layer, relevant to any retailer selling across multiple countries or languages.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Multi-market/multi-language retail sites auditing technical crawlability for both classic and AI search simultaneously; catalog-scale technical SEO where crawl budget and structured data quality are real constraints.
**Blind spots:** Deep technical-SEO expertise, not a content or brand-strategy lens — pairs with, doesn't replace, `content-strategy` or `ai-seo`'s content-side guidance.
