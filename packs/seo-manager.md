# Role Pack: SEO Manager

One page, not a new skill — a bundle of what already exists, organized around what this role actually owns: organic visibility that converts, across both traditional search and AI-mediated discovery.

## The three metrics that matter

1. **Organic revenue share** — not rankings, not traffic. What share of revenue traces back to organic, and is it growing relative to paid.
2. **AI-citation rate** — whether AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) cite this site as a source. See `ai-seo` — a newer, real metric that traditional SEO reporting misses entirely.
3. **Core Web Vitals** — LCP ≤2.5s, CLS ≤0.1, INP ≤200ms. See `website-ux` — the technical floor that determines whether any of the content work above even gets crawled and ranked properly.

## Skills this role runs

`seo-audit` (technical and on-page diagnosis), `ai-seo` (citation and AI-search optimization), `schema` (structured data), `site-architecture` (URL/category hierarchy), `website-ux` (the site-wide speed/accessibility layer every page inherits), `agent-readiness` (whether an AI *shopping* agent, not just a search engine, can transact — distinct from `ai-seo`'s citation focus), `content-strategy`, `programmatic-seo`.

## Tier 2 gates specific to this role

Per `AGENTS.md`: most of this role's work (audits, content, schema) is Tier 1. A site-wide template, navigation, or URL-structure change is Tier 2 — it affects every page at once and is expensive to reverse if wrong.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday**: Check Core Web Vitals and crawl-error trends. A regression here undercuts every other SEO effort this week, so it gets fixed first, not queued behind content work.
- **Friday**: AI-citation check on the week's highest-intent content — is it actually getting cited, and if not, what's missing relative to `ai-seo`'s extractable-structure guidance (answer blocks, FAQ schema, comparison tables)?
- **First Monday of the month**: this role owns the largest single share of the skills tagged `volatility: dated` (see `AGENTS.md`'s Skill Freshness section) — `ai-seo`, `aso`, `directory-submissions`, `schema`, `seo-audit`, `website-ux`, six of the fourteen currently tagged. When the monthly "Skill freshness review" issue is open, this is usually the role that clears most of it — check it alongside the regular Monday check, not as a separate task.

## Cross-department reporting

See `templates/cross-department-update.md` — organic revenue share and Core Web Vitals are the two numbers most legible to a non-marketing stakeholder asking "is the website doing its job."

## One win this week

Run `ai-seo` on a top product page. Add FAQ schema targeting real purchase-intent questions (not generic ones) — the fastest way to test whether this page is currently citable to an AI answer engine.
