### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - GMROI-focused inventory allocation improved profitability - worked because it directed investment toward highest-returning products aligned with target personas
   - Sell-through velocity modeling reduced stockouts - worked because it predicted demand based on actual movement rather than forecasts
   - Promotional yield optimization decreased markdowns - worked because it matched promotional depth to product elasticity and persona price sensitivity
   - ABC analysis based on persona-driven demand improved inventory efficiency - worked because it focused on the 20% of SKUs driving 80% of persona-relevant sales

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to apply uniform sell-through targets across all SKUs failed - didn't work because different persona segments have different purchasing patterns
   - Corrected by segmenting analysis by ideal customer profile from marketing strategy Sections 2-3

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major supply chain disruptions during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Back-to-school shopping increased category velocity by ~20% for relevant persona segments
   - Used inventory data enhanced with persona-tagged sales data from CRM

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/messaging accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Inventory decisions weighted heavily toward SKUs with high sell-through among Sections 3.2 and 3.4 personas; promotional strategies matched persona price points and value perceptions
   - Impact on performance: Persona-aligned inventory planning showed 35% higher GMROI and 28% lower markdowns than non-aligned approaches
