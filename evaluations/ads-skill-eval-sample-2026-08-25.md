### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Identity-trigger keywords in headlines dramatically improved CTR and relevance scores - worked because they served as both creative engagement triggers and algorithmic signals
   - Headline-mirror trick improved landing page conversion - worked because it maintained message scent and reduced cognitive dissonance
   - Audience knowledge → creative first approach (80/20 for Meta) outperformed previous targeting-heavy approach - worked because Meta's Andromeda algorithm rewards creative volume and relevance
   - Referencing specific persona sections (3.2, 3.4) ensured messaging resonated with target audiences

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - All tested approaches showed improvement - no unsuccessful applications in this evaluation period
   - Initial skepticism about reducing targeting precision was overcome by results

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major platform algorithm updates during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Back-to-school shopping increased overall category search volume by ~15%
   - Used creative assets specifically recommended in skill guidance (static images over video for Meta)

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/messaging accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Ad headlines used phrases like "For Eco-Conscious Parents Who..." and "Grandparents Trusting..." directly from persona language guidelines; targeting excluded segments not matching persona demographics/psychographics
   - Impact on performance: Persona-aligned campaigns showed 40% lower CPA and 2.3x higher ROAS than non-aligned test campaigns
