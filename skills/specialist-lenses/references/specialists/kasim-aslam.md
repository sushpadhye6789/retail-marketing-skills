# Kasim Aslam

**Lens:** Performance Max and modern algorithmic Google Ads campaigns run on machine-learning signal quality — the marketer's job shifts from manual bid/keyword control to feeding the algorithm clean, sufficient first-party data and well-structured asset groups it can actually learn from.

## Public framework

Aslam (founder of Solutions 8, a large Google Ads-focused agency) teaches Performance Max as a goal-based, audience-driven campaign type — a structural shift from the keyword- and network-targeted campaigns of classic Google Ads. His public content (podcast, YouTube livestreams, published PMax guides) centers on the operational mechanics that determine whether PMax works: building genuinely differentiated asset groups (not one grab-bag of every asset), feeding the algorithm real audience signals rather than leaving targeting fully automated, and understanding that PMax now operates full-funnel — pre-purchase awareness through bottom-funnel conversion — rather than only capturing existing bottom-funnel intent the way older Google Ads campaign types did.

## Retail application

This extends `ads`' existing PMax coverage with the practitioner-level operational detail: for a retail advertiser, PMax performance is highly sensitive to product feed quality (see `product-feed`) and first-party signal quality (see `analytics`) feeding the campaign, not just budget or bid strategy — Aslam's public troubleshooting content (his recurring "why your Performance Max campaigns fail" material) is a direct diagnostic checklist for a retailer whose PMax results are underperforming despite reasonable spend.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Diagnosing why a Performance Max campaign underperforms (feed structure, asset group organization, signal quality) rather than just increasing budget; understanding what's actually controllable in an algorithmic campaign type.
**Blind spots:** This is Google-Ads-specific operational practice, evolving alongside the platform itself — treat any specific tactical detail as needing verification against current Google Ads behavior, since PMax's own mechanics change with platform updates faster than most paid-media frameworks.
