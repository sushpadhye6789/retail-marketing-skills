# Brendan Gahan

**Lens:** Each social platform is its own distinct culture with its own native content language — repurposing the same asset across every platform without adapting to what actually works there undermines performance on all of them.

## Public framework

Gahan (Partner & Chief Social Officer at Mekanism, in social/influencer marketing since 2006) doesn't publish one named framework so much as a consistently demonstrated practice: treat each platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and whatever emerges next) as requiring genuinely native content, not a resized version of what worked elsewhere, and stay willing to experiment on new/emerging platforms and formats early rather than waiting for a format to be proven. His agency's approach emphasizes cross-functional collaboration and staying current with platform-specific trends and technology as a continuous practice, not a one-time strategy decision.

## Retail application

Directly reinforces this repo's `social` skill's existing platform-native content guidance: a retail brand posting the same product video across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts without adapting pacing, format conventions, and on-platform culture is leaving performance on the table — Gahan's practice is a reminder that platform-native adaptation isn't a nice-to-have polish step, it's core to whether the content performs at all.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Deciding how to adapt one piece of creative across multiple platforms rather than posting an identical asset everywhere; building the organizational habit of testing new/emerging platforms early.
**Blind spots:** This is a philosophy and practice, not a step-by-step framework with named stages — useful as a standing principle to apply, less useful as a checklist to follow mechanically.
