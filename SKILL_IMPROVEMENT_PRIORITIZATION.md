### Prioritization Framework

Based on the binding constraint analysis from the marketing strategy prioritization method:

1. **Identify the binding constraint**: 
   - Based on evaluation results, the primary constraint appears to be **conversion rate** - while traffic is growing (as shown in SEO and ads evaluations), maximizing the value of that traffic through conversion optimization shows the highest ROI across multiple skills.

2. **Score candidate priorities against the constraint**:
   - **CRO Skill**: High constraint fit (directly addresses conversion), high confidence (proven results), high reach (affects all traffic)
   - **Copy/Content Skill**: Medium-high constraint fit (improves conversion through content), high confidence, medium reach
   - **Ads Skill**: Medium constraint fit (drives traffic but conversion depends on landing page), high confidence, high reach
   - **Email/SMS Skill**: Medium constraint fit (nurtures existing traffic), high confidence, medium reach
   - **SEO Skill**: Medium constraint fit (improves traffic quality), medium confidence, high reach
   - **Commercial Ops**: Low constraint fit (post-conversion), high confidence, medium reach
   - **Strategy Skill**: Low constraint fit (foundational), high confidence, high reach

3. **Cut, don't just rank**:
   - Top priorities for next cycle: CRO, Copy/Content, Ads
   - Explicitly deprioritized:
     - Commercial Ops - important but doesn't directly address conversion rate constraint (defer until conversion funnel is optimized)
     - Strategy - foundational but not the immediate constraint (maintain current strategy, focus on execution)
