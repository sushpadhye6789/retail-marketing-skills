# Rand Fishkin

**Lens:** With most searches now ending without a click, marketing directly on your audience's platforms of choice — the shows they watch, the creators they follow, the communities they're in — is displacing "get found in Google search results" as the dependable growth channel.

## Public framework

Fishkin (co-founder/CEO of SparkToro, previously Moz) built **SparkToro** around **audience intelligence**: instead of guessing where an audience pays attention, directly research what YouTube channels, subreddits, websites, podcasts, and social accounts a specific audience actually engages with. This is paired with his ongoing, rigorously quantified **zero-click search** research: he first measured the phenomenon in 2019 (~50% of Google searches ending without a click), tracked it to ~65% by 2021, and SparkToro's most recent study puts it at roughly 68% in early 2026 — meaning a shrinking majority of searches ever send a visitor to a website at all, regardless of ranking position. His conclusion, stated repeatedly across interviews and SparkToro's blog: invest in brand awareness and influence on the platforms where the audience already spends time, rather than assuming SEO traffic alone can carry growth — while noting some query types (branded, local, high-intent transactional) still convert well from search specifically.

## Retail application

This is a sibling argument to `ai-seo`'s coverage of AI-mediated search (Kevin Indig's entry in this catalog covers that half) — Fishkin's zero-click research is the earlier, broader version of the same shift: search visibility alone, even ranking #1, increasingly doesn't guarantee a visitor. For retail, this reframes channel strategy: audience-intelligence research (where do *this specific* customer segment's attention actually go) should inform `channel-selection` and `local-marketing` directly, not just SEO.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Deciding where to invest attention-building effort beyond search rankings; researching a specific audience segment's actual media habits instead of assuming based on demographics.
**Blind spots:** The zero-click argument is about aggregate search behavior — it doesn't mean SEO is worthless (Fishkin himself flags branded, local, and high-intent transactional queries as still converting well from search), so this is a "don't rely on search alone" argument, not a "deprioritize search" one.
