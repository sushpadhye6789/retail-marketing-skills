# Chris Walker

**Lens:** Most B2B measurement only tracks demand *capture* (leads that already knew they wanted the product and searched or clicked) — the actual demand-*creation* activity that made a buyer want the product in the first place happens in dark social channels standard attribution can't see at all.

## Public framework

Walker (Refine Labs) draws a hard distinction between **demand creation** (building awareness and preference through content, community, and dark social — channels like private messages, forums, and third-party platforms that don't carry trackable referrer data) and **demand capture** (the search ads, retargeting, and gated-content forms that catch buyers who already decided to look). His core argument: because attribution software can only measure demand capture, B2B marketing teams systematically over-invest in capture (which shows up in dashboards) and under-invest in creation (which doesn't) — even though creation is what generates the pipeline capture later takes credit for.

## Retail application

The specific B2B mechanics (dark-social-driven MQLs, gated content) don't transfer to consumer retail, but the underlying measurement blind spot does, and it's directly relevant to this repo's `attribution` skill: a retail purchase influenced by a friend's private group-chat recommendation, a Reddit thread, or a DM screenshot of a product looks like "direct" or "unattributed" traffic in analytics — exactly the dark-social measurement gap Walker describes, just in a consumer context instead of a B2B one. The fix isn't different tooling so much as recognizing that some genuinely effective marketing activity will never show up cleanly in last-click attribution, and shouldn't be defunded just because it's hard to measure.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Explaining why a brand's "direct traffic" or "unknown source" conversions might be higher-intent than they look — dark social influence hiding inside an unattributed number; making the case for investing in unmeasurable-but-real awareness channels.
**Blind spots:** The framework is built entirely around B2B SaaS pipeline mechanics (MQLs, sales cycles, account-based measurement) — useful for a retail business's own B2B/wholesale arm directly, and as an analogy for consumer dark social, but not a literal retail measurement framework.
