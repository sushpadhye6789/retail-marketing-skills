# Justine Moore

**Lens:** The competitive edge in AI-generated video and creative isn't the generation model anymore — as generation quality converges across tools, the differentiator shifts to the editing and post-production layer that turns raw AI output into something actually usable.

## Public framework

Moore (Partner on the investing team at Andreessen Horowitz, focused on AI applications) publishes ongoing public research on generative media trends — annual consumer AI trend reports and frequent essays on a16z's platform. Her most-repeated recent argument: AI agents are quietly becoming the "invisible post-production team" for generated video — as raw generation gets commoditized, the real value-add moves to agentic editing (assembling, refining, and polishing AI-generated clips into finished creative), not the initial generation step itself. She frames this as a broader pattern across generative media: the frontier of usefulness moves downstream from generation to editing as generation itself becomes a solved, interchangeable layer.

## Retail application

Directly relevant to `ad-creative` and `video`: as AI-generated ad creative becomes cheap and widely available, a retailer's advantage won't come from having access to a generation tool (everyone will), but from the editing/curation discipline applied on top of it — matching Julia McCoy's CRAFT logic (see her entry in this catalog) for video specifically instead of text. This is more an investor's forward-looking trend read than an operational framework — useful for anticipating where ad-creative tooling is heading, less for a step-by-step process today.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Anticipating where AI ad-creative and video tooling investment is heading, to inform tool/workflow choices ahead of the market; understanding why "we have an AI video generator" alone won't be a differentiator for long.
**Blind spots:** This is a venture investor's trend analysis, not a marketer's operational playbook — genuinely useful for strategic anticipation, but doesn't include the how-to of actually running a retail video-creative workflow (pair with this repo's own `video` and `ad-creative` skills for that).
