# Savannah Sanchez

**Lens:** High-production studio ads underperform raw, platform-native UGC on social — authenticity and native format conventions (not polish) are what make an ad feel worth watching instead of skipping.

## Public framework

Sanchez (founder of The Social Savannah, a badged TikTok Marketing Partner and Meta Business Partner running 200+ ads per week across ~50 brands) built her agency's entire creative model around **rejecting AI-generated and over-produced ads in favor of high-volume, authentic UGC** — an internal team of dedicated creators and editors, with strict role separation (Strategist, Creator, Editor) and standardized briefs to produce native-feeling content at industrial scale. Her stated ratio: roughly 80% of her ads adapt an existing high-performing ad or organic trend rather than starting from a net-new concept, and she deliberately incorporates native platform elements (TikTok's comment-overlay format, native fonts, platform-specific voice effects) that signal "made for this platform" rather than "repurposed ad."

## Retail application

Directly extends `ad-creative` and `social` with a concrete production-model alternative to AI-generated ad volume (see `growth-playbooks`' AI-era Delta #3 on authenticity as premium): her 80/20 adapt-vs-create-new ratio and native-element checklist give a retailer a practical starting point for scaling UGC-style ad creative without either (a) expensive studio production or (b) generic AI-generated ads that read as inauthentic on these platforms specifically.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Building a high-volume, authentic-feeling social ad creative pipeline (TikTok/Meta specifically) without studio production costs; auditing existing ad creative for whether it reads as native to the platform or as a repurposed traditional ad.
**Blind spots:** Platform-specific tactics (native TikTok elements, format conventions) evolve quickly with the platforms themselves — treat specific format recommendations as needing a current check, and her operational model assumes access to a dedicated creator/editor pipeline that a smaller retailer may need to build or outsource differently.
