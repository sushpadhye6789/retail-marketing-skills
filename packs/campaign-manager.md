# Role Pack: Campaign Manager

One page, not a new skill — a bundle of what already exists, organized around what this role actually owns: promotions, markdowns, and trade spend that has to earn back what it costs.

## The three metrics that matter

1. **Promotional Yield** — good >5 units per $1 promotional spend, excellent >10. See `commercial-ops/references/retail-revops.md`.
2. **Trade Spend ROI** — good >1.0, strong >2.0. Same reference — measured separately from Promotional Yield, since a promo can move units well while the trade spend funding it doesn't pay back.
3. **Sell-through velocity** — category-specific; see the STR interpretation table in the same reference (>80% excellent, 40-60% moderate, <40% consider markdown or discontinuation).

## Skills this role runs

`commercial-ops` (the numbers), `discount-and-clearance` (structuring a markdown), `range-review` (what to cut before discounting it), `overstock` (diagnosing why something's not moving), `trade-and-dealer-enablement` (co-op/trade spend), `visual-merchandising` (in-store execution), `pos-marketing` (point-of-purchase).

## Tier 2 gates specific to this role

Per `AGENTS.md`'s two-tier model: a markdown beyond the brand's normal range needs an explicit margin floor (see `discount-and-clearance`'s "When to Refuse Rather Than Guess" — the skill will refuse without one, not guess). A promo launch, a new trade-spend commitment, or anything crossing a MAP/dealer-pricing line is Tier 2 — stage it, don't ship it solo.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday**: Check active promos against the Trade Spend ROI and Promotional Yield benchmarks above — not gut feel. Anything below "good" on either gets flagged before it runs another week, not after it ends.
- **Friday**: Sell-through check on anything discounted this week. Log the result — win or loss — to `.agents/marketing-learnings.md` via `compound-marketing`'s Compound stage, so next cycle's markdown decision starts smarter.

## Cross-department reporting

See `templates/cross-department-update.md` for translating "we ran a promo" into the finance/ops language a CFO or ops lead actually wants: margin impact, not just units moved.

## One win this week

Run `commercial-ops` on your last promo. Calculate its actual Promotional Yield and Trade Spend ROI — not what you assumed — before planning the next one.
