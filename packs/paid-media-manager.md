# Role Pack: Paid Media Manager

One page, not a new skill — a bundle of what already exists, organized around what this role actually owns: spending real money in a way that's provably profitable, not just a good-looking dashboard.

## The three metrics that matter

1. **Marketing ROMI** = (incremental gross profit − spend) / spend. Not ROAS — a channel can show a strong ROAS on platform-reported numbers while barely moving the business's actual profit once incrementality is accounted for.
2. **CAC** and **LTV:CAC ratio** — CAC alone is a proxy; a lower CAC that also drags down LTV (worse-fit customers) isn't a win.
3. **Incrementality** — "would this have happened anyway?" See `attribution`'s incrementality checkpoint. A platform's own attribution has a structural incentive to over-credit itself.

## Skills this role runs

`ads` (campaign strategy, targeting, bidding), `ad-creative` (the assets themselves), `attribution` (the incrementality and reconciliation layer this role depends on to know if spend is actually working), `budget-allocation` and `channel-selection` (where the money goes before it's spent).

## Tier 2 gates specific to this role

Per `AGENTS.md`: any budget shift beyond ~10%, a new audience beyond ~20% of spend, or a hero-creative change is Tier 2 — stage it for approval, don't ship it unilaterally. Drafting creative, running analysis, and testing within existing budget/audience parameters is Tier 1.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday**: Budget pacing check against plan — see `budget-pacing-tracker`. Catch a channel drifting off-plan before Friday, not after the week's spend is already committed.
- **Friday**: Marketing ROMI validation before next week's spend decisions. A/B discipline applies here too — test one variable at a time, run to real significance, kill losers fast rather than letting an inconclusive test run indefinitely.

## Cross-department reporting

See `templates/cross-department-update.md` — Marketing ROMI and incrementality are the two things that actually answer a CFO's "prove this spend is working" question; ROAS and CTR don't.

## One win this week

Before the next ad launch, run the same commercial discipline `commercial-ops` applies to a promo: validate the numbers first. Check this channel's actual incrementality, not just its platform-reported ROAS, before committing next week's budget to it.
