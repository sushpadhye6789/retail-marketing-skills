# Retail RevOps Framework Reference

## Core Retail Financial Mechanics

### GMROI (Gross Margin Return on Investment)

**Formula**: GMROI = (Gross Margin $) / (Average Inventory Cost $)

**Interpretation**:
- GMROI > 1.0: Inventory investment generates gross profit
- GMROI > 2.0: Healthy performance for most retail categories
- GMROI > 3.0: Strong performance (target for many retailers)
- GMROI < 1.0: Inventory investment loses money

**Calculation Example**:
- Gross Margin: $500,000
- Average Inventory Cost: $200,000
- GMROI = $500,000 / $200,000 = 2.5

**Improvement Levers**:
1. Increase gross margin through better pricing or lower COGS
2. Reduce average inventory while maintaining sales
3. Increase sales velocity through better assortment and presentation

### Sell-Through Rate (STR)

**Formula**: STR = (Units Sold) / (Beginning Inventory + Receipts) × 100

**Interpretation**:
- STR > 80%: Excellent sell-through (fast-moving categories)
- STR 60-80%: Good performance
- STR 40-60%: Moderate (may need markdown support)
- STR < 40%: Poor (consider discontinuation or deep markdown)

**Weekly STR Tracking**:
- Week 1: 15% (new receipt)
- Week 2: 35% 
- Week 3: 55%
- Week 4: 70% (reorder trigger at 65-70%)
- Week 5: 80% (consider additional receipt)
- Week 6+: Monitor for aging

### Promotional Yield

**Formula**: Promotional Yield = (Incremental Units Sold during Promotion) / (Promotional Investment $)

**Components**:
- Incremental Units: Units sold above baseline during promo period
- Promotional Investment: Total cost of promo (discounts, advertising, labor)

**Benchmark**: 
- Good: > 5 units per $1 promotional spend
- Excellent: > 10 units per $1 promotional spend
- Poor: < 2 units per $1 promotional spend

### Trade Spend ROI

**Formula**: Trade Spend ROI = [(Incremental Sales $ × Gross Margin %) − Trade Spend $] / Trade Spend $

**Components**:
- Incremental Sales $: Revenue above baseline attributable to the trade spend (co-op advertising, slotting, promotional funding paid to or through a dealer/retailer — see `trade-and-dealer-enablement`)
- Gross Margin %: Applied to incremental sales, not total sales — trade spend earns back margin dollars, not top-line revenue
- Trade Spend $: Total dollars committed, not just the discount itself — includes co-op ad funding, display fees, and any promotional allowance paid out

**Benchmark**:
- Good: ROI > 1.0 (the spend pays for itself in incremental margin)
- Strong: ROI > 2.0 (target for most trade-spend programs)
- Poor: ROI < 0.5 (reconsider the program or the dealer relationship it's funding)

**Distinct from Promotional Yield**: Promotional Yield measures unit-movement efficiency of a consumer-facing promotion (discounts, advertising aimed at the end customer). Trade Spend ROI measures dollar efficiency of money paid to or through a channel partner (co-op ads, slotting, dealer promotional funding) — the two often run alongside each other in a single campaign and should be evaluated separately, since a promotion can move plenty of units (good Promotional Yield) while the trade spend funding it doesn't pay back (poor Trade Spend ROI) if too much of the spend went to co-op fees rather than the discount itself.

### Inventory Turns

**Formula**: Inventory Turns = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value

**Interpretation by Category**:
- Grocery/consumables: 12-24 turns per year
- Apparel/fashion: 4-6 turns per year
- Home goods: 3-4 turns per year
- Luxury goods: 2-3 turns per year
- Electronics: 6-8 turns per year

**Improvement Strategies**:
1. Better demand forecasting
2. Improved allocation to high-performing doors
3. Faster replenishment of best sellers
4. Aggressive markdown of slow movers

### Units per Transaction (UPT)

**Formula**: UPT = Total Units Sold / Number of Transactions

**Category Benchmarks**:
- Convenience/grocery: 1.2-1.8 UPT
- Drug/pharmacy: 1.5-2.5 UPT
- Specialty retail: 2.0-3.5 UPT
- Department stores: 3.0-5.0 UPT
- Off-price retailers: 4.0-6.0 UPT

**UPT Improvement Tactics**:
1. Cross-merchandising complementary products
2. Bundle promotions (buy 2, get 10% off)
3. Impulse items at checkout
4. Staff selling techniques and suggestions

### Average Transaction Value (ATV)

**Formula**: ATV = Total Sales $ / Number of Transactions

**Category Benchmarks**:
- Convenience stores: $8-$15 ATV
- Drug stores: $15-$25 ATV
- Specialty apparel: $50-$100 ATV
- Electronics: $100-$300 ATV
- Luxury goods: $200-$1000+ ATV

**ATV Improvement Strategies**:
1. Upselling and bundling
2. Loyalty programs encouraging larger baskets
3. Free shipping thresholds (ecommerce)
4. Product placement encouraging add-ons

## Retail-Specific Processes

### Assortment Planning Framework

**Pre-Season Planning Steps**:
1. **Performance Review** (prior year):
   - GMROI by category/sub-category/SKU
   - Sell-through rate analysis
   - Inventory turns and aging
   - Margin and velocity correlation

2. **Demand Forecasting**:
   - Historical sales trends
   - Market trend analysis
   - Weather and event impact modeling
   - Planned promotional calendar

3. **Assortment Strategy**:
   - Core vs. fashion vs. promotional balance
   - Price point distribution
   - Size/color/attribute allocation
   - New item vs. carryover ratio

4. **Financial Planning**:
   - Open-to-buy budget by month
   - Receipt planning based on lead times
   - Markdown budget allocation
   - Gross margin targets

**In-Season Management**:
- **Weekly OTB Review**:
  - Actual vs. planned sales
  - Receipts vs. plan
  - Ending inventory position
  - Open-to-buy for future weeks

- **Replenishment Triggers**:
  - Base replenishment on sell-through velocity
  - Safety stock for variability
  - Maximum stock levels to prevent overbuying
  - Store-level vs. DC-level allocation rules

- **Markdown Decision Process**:
  - Identify slow sellers (STR < category avg for 3+ weeks)
  - Analyze root cause (price, presentation, external factors)
  - Determine optimal markdown depth
  - Execute and monitor results
  - Analyze post-markdown performance

### Wholesale Distribution Operations

**Wholesale Pipeline Management**:
1. **Lead Generation & Qualification**:
   - Retail buyer identification (chain vs. independent)
   - Territory and account segmentation
   - Initial outreach and needs assessment

2. **Assortment Presentation**:
   - Digital line sheets or physical sample kits
   - Margin and sell-through projections
   - Marketing support and co-op programs
   - Logistics and delivery capabilities

3. **Order Negotiation & Fulfillment**:
   - Opening order terms and conditions
   - Co-op advertising and promotional support
   - Delivery windows and OTIF (On-Time In-Full)
   - Payment terms and credit management

4. **In-Store Execution Support**:
   - Visual merchandising guidelines
   - Sales associate training materials
   - Point-of-sale materials and shelf talkers
   - Replenishment and allocation guidance

5. **Performance Monitoring**:
   - Weekly sell-through reporting by door
   - Reorder rate analysis
   - Return and deduction management
   - Quarterly business reviews

**Key Wholesale Metrics Dashboard**:
- **Door Performance**:
  - Units per store per week (velocity)
  - Reorder rate (% of doors reordering)
  - Sell-through % by week
  - Average inventory on hand

- **Financial Performance**:
  - Gross margin % achieved
  - Promotional effectiveness (lift vs. baseline)
  - Markdown rate and effectiveness
  - Return rate and reasons

- **Operational Excellence**:
  - OTIF delivery performance
  - Invoice accuracy and deductions
  - Damage and defect rates
  - Compliance with routing guides

### D2C & Ecommerce Operations

**Fulfillment Center Metrics**:
- **Order Accuracy**: % of orders picked, packed, and shipped correctly
- **Order Cycle Time**: Average time from order receipt to shipment
- **Dock-to-Stock Time**: Time from receipt to available for picking
- **Inventory Accuracy**: System vs. physical count variance

**Customer Experience Metrics**:
- **Conversion Rate**: % of site visitors who make a purchase
- **Cart Abandonment Rate**: % of carts abandoned before checkout
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Total revenue / number of orders
- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: % of customers who buy again within timeframe
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Marketing spend / new customers
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Predicted net profit from customer relationship

**Returns Management**:
- **Return Rate**: % of orders returned
- **Return Reason Analysis**:
  - Size/fit issues
  - Product not as described
  - Changed mind/no longer wanted
  - Defective/damaged
  - Late arrival
- **Restocking Fee Impact**: Effect on return behavior
- **Resellable Return Rate**: % of returns that can be resold as new

## Data Architecture & Reporting

### Essential Retail Data Elements

**Product Master Data**:
- SKU/UPC
- Description and attributes
- Cost and retail pricing
- Dimensions and weight
- Country of origin
- Harmonized tariff code
- Season and launch date
- Expected lifecycle length

**Inventory Data**:
- On-hand quantity by location
- On-order quantity (POs not received)
- Allocated quantity (committed to orders)
- In-transit quantity (shipped but not received)
- Available quantity (on-hand - allocated)
- Safety stock levels
- Reorder point and quantity

**Transaction Data**:
- Sales timestamp and location
- Quantity and extended amount
- Discount amount and type
- Tax and shipping charges
- Payment method and tender type
- Customer ID (if loyalty program)
- Sales associate/employee ID

**Customer Data** (for loyalty programs):
- Demographics and contact info
- Purchase history and frequency
- Average basket size and spend
- Preferred channels and categories
- Engagement with marketing communications
- Lifetime value and churn risk

### Retail Reporting Calendar

**Daily Reports**:
- Flash sales report (previous day sales vs. plan)
- Inventory receipts and discrepancies
- Exception reporting (negative sales, voids, refunds)
- Top/bottom selling SKUs

**Weekly Reports**:
- Sell-through analysis by category/SKU
- GMROI and inventory turns by department
- Promotional performance vs. plan
- Aging inventory report (by weeks on hand)
- Open-to-buy position and projections
- Vendor performance (OTD, fill rate, accuracy)

**Monthly Reports**:
- Category profit and loss statement
- Markdown effectiveness and margin impact
- New item performance (first 8 weeks)
- Private label vs. national brand performance
- Sales per square foot and sales per labor hour
- Shrinkage and operational metrics
- Cash conversion cycle and working capital

**Quarterly Reports**:
- Assortment productivity analysis
- Customer segmentation and migration
- Competitive share and pricing analysis
- Capital expenditure review and planning
- Strategic initiative progress tracking
- Executive summary and business review

## Process Workflows

### New Product Launch Process

**Phase 1: Concept to Sample (8-12 weeks pre-launch)**:
1. **Idea Generation**: Market trends, customer feedback, competitive analysis
2. **Concept Screening**: Strategic fit, margin potential, manufacturing feasibility
3. **Development**: Specifications, samples, costing, packaging
4. **Sample Review**: Cross-functional evaluation (design, merchandising, quality)
5. **Pre-Launch Buy Decision**: Based on sell-through projections and category strategy
6. **Initial Order**: Factory commitment and production scheduling

**Phase 2: Pre-Launch Preparation (4-8 weeks pre-launch)**:
1. **Marketing Planning**: Launch calendar, promotional support, PR strategy
2. **Sales Enablement**: Line sheets, training materials, objection handling
3. **Logistics Planning**: Receipt scheduling, allocation strategy, DC preparation
4. **Visual Merchandising**: Planograms, fixture requirements, display concepts
5. **Systems Setup**: SKU creation, pricing, barcode assignment, EDI testing

**Phase 3: Launch Execution (Launch week)**:
1. **Receipt and Putaway**: DC receiving, quality check, inventory putaway
2. **Store Allocation**: Based on OTB and historical performance
3. **In-Store Execution**: Fixture setup, product placement, signage
4. **Staff Training**: Product knowledge, selling points, promotional details
5. **Marketing Activation**: Launch advertising, social media, in-store promotions
6. **Launch Monitoring**: Early sales feedback, inventory levels, customer response

**Phase 4: Post-Launch Analysis (4-8 weeks post-launch)**:
1. **Early Sell-Through Review**: First 2-4 weeks performance vs. plan
2. **Replenishment Decision**: Based on velocity and sell-through
3. **Quality and Feedback Review**: Customer returns, defects, vendor performance
4. **Financial Analysis**: Actual vs. planned margin, GMROI, inventory turns
5. **Launch Assessment**: Go/No-Go for continued production or expansion
6. **Lessons Learned**: Documentation for future launches

### Markdown Optimization Process

**Weekly Markdown Review Cycle**:
1. **Identification** (Monday):
   - Run STR report for all SKUs
   - Flag SKUs with STR < category average for 3+ weeks
   - Prioritize by inventory dollars at risk and age

2. **Analysis** (Tuesday-Wednesday):
   - Root cause analysis (price, assortment, presentation, external factors)
   - Competitive price check and market positioning
   - Customer feedback and review analysis
   - Seasonal relevance and trend alignment

3. **Decision** (Thursday):
   - Select markdown candidates for next week
   - Determine markdown percentage (10%, 20%, 30%, 50%, etc.)
   - Calculate margin impact and breakeven sell-through lift required
   - Schedule promotional support if needed (feature, display, advertising)

4. **Execution** (Friday-Sunday):
   - Price changes in POS system
   - Update shelf tags and signage
   - Execute promotional displays and feature placement
   - Notify sales staff of markdown items and selling points

5. **Post-Markdown Analysis** (Following Monday):
   - Measure sell-through lift vs. baseline
   - Calculate actual margin earned vs. projected
   - Analyze cannibalization effects on related SKUs
   - Determine if additional markdown needed or return to regular price
   - Document learnings for future similar situations

### Wholesale Account Management

**Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Framework**:
1. **Performance Review** (Past Quarter):
   - Sales vs. plan and prior year
   - Sell-through velocity and reorder rates
   - Gross margin achievement and promotional effectiveness
   - Inventory levels and turns at retail
   - Returns, damages, and chargebacks analysis

2. **Opportunity Identification**:
   - Assortment gaps and white space analysis
   - Seasonal and event-driven opportunities
   - Cross-selling and bundling potential
   - Geographic expansion within account
   - New store door opportunities

3. **Planning** (Next Quarter):
   - Assortment adjustments based on performance
   - Promotional calendar and trade spend allocation
   - Receipt planning and allocation strategy
   - Marketing and merchandising support levels
   - Logistics and delivery schedule confirmation

4. **Negotiation and Agreement**:
   - Pricing and terms for next period
   - Co-op funding levels and approval process
   - Delivery schedules and OTIF expectations
   - Performance guarantees and penalties
   - Review and sign updated agreements

**Monthly Account Management**:
- **Performance Tracking**: Weekly sell-through and inventory reports
- **Issue Resolution**: Address out-of-stocks, overstocks, and damages
- **Replenishment Guidance**: Based on sell-through and OTB positions
- **Promotional Execution**: Ensure co-op funds are properly utilized
- **Relationship Building**: Regular communication and store visits as needed
