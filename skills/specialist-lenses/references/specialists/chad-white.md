# Chad S. White

**Lens:** Email deliverability isn't a technical afterthought bolted onto a campaign — it's a direct revenue lever, and treating lifecycle email as customer service first (relevant, well-targeted, permission-respecting) is what protects the sender reputation that determines whether campaigns even reach the inbox.

## Public framework

White (Head of Research at Oracle Digital Experience Agency, author of four editions of *Email Marketing Rules*) has published nearly 4,000 posts of deliverability and lifecycle-email research. His consistent public argument, backed by Oracle's own client data: deliverability differences translate directly to revenue — the industry averages roughly 85% deliverability, while disciplined deliverability practice can push that toward 97%, a gap he quantifies as worth over a million dollars annually for a $10M-revenue email program. His broader philosophy treats lifecycle communications (welcome flows, browse/cart abandonment, post-purchase, win-back) as customer-experience touchpoints that should earn continued permission through relevance, not as a blast-promo channel to be maximized until subscribers complain or unsubscribe.

## Retail application

Directly extends this repo's `emails` skill: White's deliverability-as-revenue framing gives concrete stakes to disciplines `emails` already covers (list hygiene, segmentation, sending cadence) — a retailer sending frequent promotional blasts without deliverability discipline is measurably leaving revenue on the table through suppressed inbox placement, not just risking a vague "reputation" concern.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Making the business case for deliverability investment (list hygiene, sender reputation, engagement-based segmentation) in concrete revenue terms; auditing whether a lifecycle email program treats subscribers as customers or as a blast list.
**Blind spots:** Deliverability specifics (sender reputation mechanics, ISP-level filtering behavior) change over time and by provider — treat any specific percentage or mechanism as needing a current check against his latest research rather than assuming it's stable.
