# Friday Review

Run at the end of the week via `claude -p` or as a Friday session's first message. Closes the loop `monday-plan.md` opened — this is the Compound step (see `compound-marketing`) applied at the week level, not just per-campaign.

## What to read first

1. Whichever `packs/*.md` file matches the role running this — its "Weekly rhythm" Friday item.
2. Any `.agents/execution-log.md` entries from this week (see `AGENTS.md`'s execution-receipt convention) — what Tier 2 actions actually happened.

## Generate

A short (under 250 words) review covering:

- **This role's Friday metric check**, from the matching pack, stated with the actual number against its benchmark — not "trending well."
- **Win or loss, plainly stated.** Per `compound-marketing`'s Optimise stage: keep, kill, or "inconclusive, needs more signal" — don't force a verdict the data doesn't support.
- **One learnings entry**, using `compound-marketing/references/learning-entry-template.md`, appended to `.agents/marketing-learnings.md` — even on a loss, especially on a loss. A week that ends without a learnings entry is a week that didn't compound.
- **The cross-department update**, if one's owed this week — see `templates/cross-department-update.md`.

## What this is not

Not a performance review of the person running it. A week where the honest review is "we don't have enough signal yet to call this" is a successful review, not a failed one — forcing a premature verdict here is worse than admitting the data isn't in yet.
