# Role Pack: Product / Merchandising Manager

One page, not a new skill — a bundle of what already exists, organized around what this role actually owns: assortment, product presentation, and the post-purchase experience that turns a first sale into a second one.

## The three metrics that matter

1. **Repeat purchase rate** — the metric `post-purchase-experience` and `retention-and-winback` are built to move. A first sale funded by acquisition spend that never repeats is the most expensive kind of revenue.
2. **Attachment rate / AOV** — see `product-page`'s attachment mechanics: prompts that complete an obviously incomplete purchase convert better than generic upsells.
3. **GMROI** — good >2.0, strong >3.0 (see `commercial-ops/references/retail-revops.md`). The metric that ties assortment decisions back to whether the inventory investment actually pays back.

## Skills this role runs

`product-page` (PDP/PLP structure and merchandising copy), `product-feed` (the underlying data quality everything else depends on), `product-lifecycle` (stage-appropriate positioning), `post-purchase-experience` (unboxing through repurchase triggers), `offers` (bundling and attachment), `price-elasticity` (sizing a price or discount against actual margin, not a guess), `range-review` (what to keep, cut, or expand).

## Tier 2 gates specific to this role

Per `AGENTS.md`: an assortment change beyond routine range management, or a new product launch, is Tier 2. Routine PDP copy and content updates are Tier 1.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday**: Range-review check — any category drifting toward the STR "poor" threshold (<40%, per `commercial-ops/references/retail-revops.md`) gets a markdown or discontinuation decision this week, not next quarter.
- **Friday**: Repeat purchase rate and attachment rate check against the prior period. A size/fit or quality issue showing up in returns data (see `post-purchase-experience`) is worth surfacing to `product-page` before next week's copy, not filed away.

## Cross-department reporting

See `templates/cross-department-update.md` — this role's numbers (GMROI, inventory turns) are usually what finance actually wants from a "how's the assortment doing" update.
