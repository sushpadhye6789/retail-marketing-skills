# Thomas Petit

**Lens:** Mobile app growth metrics that look right in aggregate (blended ROAS, view-through conversions) often mislead for subscription apps specifically, because they hide which users actually stick around versus churn immediately after the trial.

## Public framework

Petit is an independent mobile growth consultant (Deezer, Lingokids, and others) and frequent speaker (App Growth Summit, App Promotion Summit, Mobile Growth Europe). His recurring public argument — the subject of multiple podcast appearances — is that **view-through attribution and target-ROAS bidding don't work well for subscription apps** specifically, because they optimize toward the moment of install/trial-start rather than the moment that actually matters for a subscription business: whether the user converts to paid and retains. He advocates evaluating app-store-optimization (ASO) and paid user-acquisition performance against post-trial retention and paid conversion, not top-of-funnel install volume.

## Retail application

Only applies where the retail business has its own shopping or loyalty app with a genuine subscription or membership component (subscribe-and-save, a paid loyalty tier) — this extends this repo's `aso` skill with a warning specific to that case: an app's install/download numbers or view-through-attributed conversions can look healthy while the actual subscription business underneath is churning fast, because standard mobile-marketing metrics weren't built to catch that.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** A retailer with a subscription-model app (subscribe-and-save, paid membership tier) auditing whether app marketing metrics are measuring the right outcome; app store optimization for a retail app specifically.
**Blind spots:** Narrow, genuinely niche fit — irrelevant to a retailer without a dedicated mobile app, and even for retailers with an app, only directly applicable if there's a subscription/recurring-revenue component to it. Flagged Low retail fit for exactly this reason; use only when the specific situation matches.
