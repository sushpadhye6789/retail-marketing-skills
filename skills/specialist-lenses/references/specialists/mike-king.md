# Mike King

**Lens:** A single question typed into an AI search engine isn't one search — it's dozens or hundreds of synthetic sub-queries running in parallel behind the scenes, and content only gets retrieved into the final answer if it satisfies enough of those sub-queries, not just the visible one.

## Public framework

King (founder/CEO of iPullRank, named 2025 Search Marketer of the Year) developed **Relevance Engineering**, a methodology combining vector embeddings, cosine similarity, and semantic scoring to determine whether content will actually surface during AI-driven retrieval. His related public research on **query fan-out** — the process by which AI systems expand one user query into many related, implicit, comparative, and personalized sub-queries running simultaneously — has become one of the most-cited explanations of how Google's AI Mode and similar systems actually decide what to retrieve and cite. His practical advice: build content that answers the fan-out (the adjacent and implicit questions a user didn't type but the AI system infers), not just the literal query.

## Retail application

This directly extends `ai-seo` with the retrieval mechanics underneath Kevin Indig's brand-visibility argument (see that entry in this catalog): a retailer's product or category page needs to satisfy the *fanned-out* version of a shopper's question ("best waterproof hiking boots" implicitly fans out into sub-queries about price range, brand comparisons, specific use cases, sizing) — structuring content to answer that fuller cluster, not just the headline query, is what determines AI-retrieval visibility.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Structuring product and category content to be retrieved by AI search systems specifically, not just rank in classic search; technical/content teams deciding what implicit sub-questions a page needs to answer.
**Blind spots:** Genuinely technical methodology (vector embeddings, semantic scoring) — implementing it well typically needs technical SEO or content-engineering capability beyond a marketing team alone; treat as the target content should hit, with `seo-audit`/`ai-seo` for the execution path to get there.
