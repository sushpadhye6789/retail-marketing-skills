# Paul Roetzer

**Lens:** AI adoption fails when it's ad hoc — a tool trialed here, a pilot there, with no connection to actual business objectives — so AI initiatives need the same structured, strategic evaluation as any other major marketing investment, not enthusiasm-driven experimentation.

## Public framework

Roetzer (founder/CEO of Marketing AI Institute) developed the **AI Marketing Canvas**, a structured framework guiding organizations through systematically identifying, evaluating, and implementing AI opportunities across marketing functions — from research and segmentation through content creation, campaign optimization, and CRM — rather than adopting tools reactively. His broader public work (the Marketing AI Institute's tiered course series — AI Fundamentals, Piloting AI, Scaling AI — plus his book *Marketing Artificial Intelligence* and *The Artificial Intelligence Show* podcast) consistently emphasizes staged adoption: understand before piloting, pilot before scaling.

## Retail application

Directly relevant to this repo's own premise — a retail marketing team adopting AI tools (this skill repo included) benefits from the same staged discipline Roetzer teaches: map which specific marketing functions (content drafting, campaign analysis, personalization) actually have a real AI opportunity and business case, rather than adopting AI tools function-by-function without an overall plan. Pairs well with `compound-marketing`'s Brief stage — naming the actual outcome an AI-assisted workflow should move, not just "using AI" as the goal itself.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Building a structured, prioritized AI-adoption roadmap across marketing functions rather than piecemeal tool adoption; making the business case for AI investment to non-technical stakeholders.
**Blind spots:** A strategic-planning and adoption-sequencing framework, not a technical implementation guide — useful for deciding *what* to adopt and *when*, not *how* to build a specific AI workflow.
