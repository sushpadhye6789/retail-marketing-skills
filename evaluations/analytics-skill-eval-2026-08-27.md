### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Comprehensive tracking plan worked because it focused on decision-relevant events rather than trying to track everything
   - Data validation procedures worked because they caught implementation errors early before they corrupted decision-making
   - UTM strategy worked because it provided consistent channel tracking across platforms
   - Privacy-compliant implementation worked because it built trust with customers while maintaining data quality

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to track every possible interaction underperformed - didn't work because it created noise and made it hard to find meaningful signals
   - Corrected by focusing on events that directly inform marketing decisions (per skill principle #1)

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major platform policy changes during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Prepared for back-to-school shopping period (needed accurate tracking for promotional campaigns)
   - Used customer research insights to inform what events to track (e.g., persona-specific content engagement)

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did analytics setup accurately reflect the need to measure ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Implemented persona-based custom dimensions and segmentation; tracked engagement with persona-specific content; validated data quality for decision-making about preferred segments
   - Impact on performance: Data-driven decisions about persona-targeted initiatives showed 2.8x higher ROI than non-data-driven decisions
