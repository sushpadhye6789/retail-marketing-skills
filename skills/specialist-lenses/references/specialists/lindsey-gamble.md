# Lindsey Gamble

**Lens:** Creators are increasingly media companies and product-development partners in their own right, not a one-off ad placement — brands that integrate them earlier and more deeply (co-branded product, equity, ongoing collaboration) get more durable value than brands that just book a single sponsored post.

## Public framework

Gamble (VP, Creator Strategy & Innovation at IZEA; a recognized LinkedIn Top Voice in the creator economy) doesn't publish one named framework — his contribution is a widely-read, curated newsletter and consulting practice tracking creator-economy infrastructure trends: how platforms, tools, and deal structures are evolving to treat creators as durable business partners (equity stakes, co-branded commerce, ongoing product input) rather than one-time media buys. His consistent editorial position, reflected across his newsletter picks, is that the creator economy is maturing from ad-hoc sponsored content toward structured, infrastructure-supported partnerships.

## Retail application

Extends this repo's `influencer-marketing` skill with the trend-forward view: a retail brand's creator strategy built only around one-off sponsored posts is already behind where the category is heading — Gamble's consistent theme suggests the more durable version involves creators earlier (product co-development, ongoing ambassador relationships, revenue-share or equity structures) rather than transactional one-off deals, echoed in `growth-playbooks`' Community-Led & UGC Flywheels entry (Gymshark's early creator seeding).
