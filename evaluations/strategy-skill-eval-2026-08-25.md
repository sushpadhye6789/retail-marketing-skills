### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Deep customer research synthesis improved persona accuracy - worked because it was based on verbatim customer language and observed behavior
   - Strategic priorities based on quantitative analysis (CLV, CAC) improved resource allocation - worked because it focused effort on highest-returning activities
   - Clear linkage between strategy and tactical execution improved consistency - worked because downstream skills had explicit references to follow
   - Regular strategy review cadence improved market responsiveness - worked because it allowed quick pivots based on performance data

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to create strategy without sufficient customer research underperformed - didn't work because it relied on assumptions rather than validated insights
   - Corrected by grounding all strategy elements in customer research data from Sections 2-3

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major market disruptions during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Prepared for back-to-school and holiday shopping seasons through strategic timing
   - Used customer service transcripts, social listening, and purchase data to enhance persona accuracy

4. **Personas alignment check**:
