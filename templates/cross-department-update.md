# Cross-Department Update Template

Shared by all five role packs in `packs/`. Answers the pain point that started this whole enhancement effort: "I couldn't easily respond to other departments when they asked for updates on what we had done in marketing." Finance, ops, and sales don't want a campaign recap — they want the same four things, in their language.

## The four questions every stakeholder actually asks

1. **What did marketing actually spend, and what did it return?** Not "we ran 10 campaigns" — Marketing ROMI, or the role-specific equivalent (Promotional Yield / Trade Spend ROI for a Campaign Manager, GMROI for Merchandising). If the honest answer this period is "we don't have a confident number yet," say that — a fabricated number is worse than an honest gap.
2. **What's the incremental impact, not the gross activity?** "Would this have happened anyway?" (see `attribution`'s incrementality checkpoint) is the question a CFO is actually asking when they ask "what did marketing do this quarter" — they don't want a list of outputs, they want to know what wouldn't have happened without marketing.
3. **What's the risk or exposure right now?** Inventory tied up in a slow-moving category, a dealer relationship strained by a channel-conflict issue, a compliance question still open — the thing ops or legal would want flagged before it becomes a bigger problem.
4. **What decision, if any, needs a stakeholder outside marketing?** A Tier 2 action (see `AGENTS.md`) that needs budget sign-off, a legal review, or a channel-partner conversation — named specifically, not buried in a status update nobody reads closely enough to catch it.

## Format

```markdown
## [Role] update — [date range]

**Return**: [the role-specific headline metric, with the actual number — not "positive trend"]
**Incremental**: [what wouldn't have happened without this work, in one sentence]
**Watch**: [the one risk or exposure worth knowing about, or "nothing flagged"]
**Needs a decision**: [the specific Tier 2 ask, who it's for, and by when — or "nothing pending"]
```

## What this replaces

The instinct to write a narrative recap of everything that happened. A stakeholder outside marketing doesn't need the narrative — they need the four answers above, in under 30 seconds of reading. If there's a genuinely interesting story behind one of the four answers, that's a follow-up conversation, not something to front-load into the update itself.
