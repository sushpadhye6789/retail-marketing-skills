### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Persona-focused keyword research improved rankings - worked because it targeted specific language and intent from Sections 3.2 and 3.4
   - Technical SEO improvements boosted rankings - worked because they addressed site speed and mobile usability (confirmed ranking factors)
   - Content clusters built topical authority - worked because they signaled expertise to search engines through comprehensive coverage
   - FAQ schema implementation increased visibility - worked because it provided direct answers to customer questions in SERP

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to target broad, high-volume keywords underperformed - didn't work because they were too competitive and didn't match specific persona intent
   - Corrected by focusing on long-tail, question-based keywords from customer research that matched persona language

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major Google algorithm updates during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Increased organic traffic during back-to-school research period (August)
   - Used customer research insights enhanced with verbatim language from grill-me interviews for content creation

4. **Personas/search intent alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/content accurately reflect the search intent of ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Blog content used phrases like "How to choose safe baby products without chemicals..." and "Best eco-friendly options for budget-conscious grandparents..."; product pages answered specific questions from persona research
   - Impact on performance: Persona-aligned content showed 2.8x higher engagement time and 1.9x higher conversion rates than generic SEO targets
