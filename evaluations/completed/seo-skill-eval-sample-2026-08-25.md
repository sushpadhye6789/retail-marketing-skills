### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Persona-focused keyword research improved rankings - worked because it targeted specific language and intent from Sections 3.2 and 3.4
   - Technical SEO improvements boosted rankings - worked because site speed and mobile usability are confirmed ranking factors
   - Content clusters built topical authority - worked because comprehensive coverage signals expertise to search engines
   - Structured data implementation increased CTR - worked because rich snippets stand out in SERPs

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to target broad, high-volume keywords failed - didn't work because high competition made ranking difficult
   - Corrected by focusing on long-tail, persona-specific keywords with lower competition but higher intent

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major Google algorithm updates during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Increased search volume for "fall baby clothes" and "holiday gifts for babies" by ~35%
   - Used Google Search Console data enhanced with persona-tagged search queries from customer service
