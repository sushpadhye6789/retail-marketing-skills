# Jason Goldberg

**Lens:** The specific channel (in-store, mobile app, website, marketplace) matters less to a shopper than whether the overall path to purchase is frictionless — measure and optimize for removing friction across the whole buying journey, not for making any one channel look good in isolation.

## Public framework

Goldberg ("Retailgeek," Chief Commerce Strategy Officer at Publicis Groupe, a 4th-generation retailer who built one of the first ecommerce sites for Blockbuster in 1995) has spent decades of public commentary (his long-running "Jason & Scot Show" podcast, RetailWire and Forbes columns) arguing that **removing friction from the buying experience** — online and offline both — is the through-line that actually predicts retail success, more than any single channel's individual performance. He's consistently critical of retailers who optimize channels as silos (a great website, a separate and disconnected in-store experience) rather than one continuous customer journey.

## Retail application

This is a direct extension of this repo's own cross-channel-consistency principle (already captured in `.agents/expert-notes.md`'s private operating notes on website/POS/ads/eDM consistency): Goldberg's specific framing — measure friction across the *entire* path to purchase, not channel-by-channel performance — gives that principle a concrete diagnostic lens. A retailer with a fast checkout online but a slow, disconnected in-store pickup experience (BOPIS) has a friction problem the channel-level metrics won't surface.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Auditing a retailer's full purchase journey for friction points that fall between channels (online-to-in-store handoffs, app-to-website continuity) rather than optimizing each channel independently.
**Blind spots:** A diagnostic principle and industry-commentary practice, not a step-by-step implementation framework — useful for identifying where friction exists, pairs with this repo's channel-specific skills (`cro`, `pos-marketing`, `local-marketing`) for the actual fixes.
