### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Persona-specific welcome series improved engagement - worked because it addressed specific needs and language from Sections 3.2 and 3.4
   - Post-purchase nurture sequence increased repeat purchases - worked because it provided value beyond the purchase and built product confidence
   - Win-back campaign with targeted offers reactivated lapsed customers - worked because it addressed specific reasons for lapse identified in customer research
   - Behavioral trigger emails performed better than batch blasts - worked because they were timely and relevant to user actions

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to use generic welcome series for all new subscribers underperformed - didn't work because different persona segments had different informational needs
   - Corrected by creating segment-specific welcome series based on marketing strategy personas

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major email deliverability policy changes during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Increased engagement during back-to-school shopping period (August)
   - Used email engagement data enhanced with persona-tagged open/click behavior from CRM

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did targeting/messaging accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from marketing strategy? [Y]
   - Evidence: Subject lines and copy used phrases like "For Eco-Conscious Parents..." and "Special Offer for Grandparents..." directly from persona language; product recommendations matched persona preferences
   - Impact on performance: Persona-aligned email campaigns showed 2.3x higher revenue per email than non-aligned blasts
