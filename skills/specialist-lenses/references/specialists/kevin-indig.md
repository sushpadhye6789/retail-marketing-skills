# Kevin Indig

**Lens:** AI-mediated search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) is a brand channel disguised as a performance channel — the unit of optimization is whether an AI names, trusts, and recommends your brand, not whether a page ranks for a keyword.

## Public framework

Indig publishes **Growth Memo** (~21,000 subscribers), the most-cited research source in the generative-engine-optimization (GEO) field, built on original data studies rather than opinion. His **"State of AI Search Optimization"** annual report and ongoing research quantify how AI answer engines actually select and cite sources — a July 2026 behavioral study following real users through 221 ChatGPT shopping tasks found brands selected by the AI captured roughly 24% share of voice versus 11% for brands the AI passed over, and separate citation analysis found a large share of ChatGPT citations draw from early in a page rather than evenly across it. His **Visibility Layer and Trust Stack** framework turns this research into an operating model: treat AI recommendation the way you'd treat brand consideration, not the way you'd treat a keyword ranking.

## Retail application

This directly extends this repo's `ai-seo` skill with the "why it matters at the brand level" argument: a retailer showing up (or not) when a customer asks an AI agent "best waterproof hiking boots under $200" is now a real, measurable acquisition channel with its own selection dynamics — different from classic SEO's crawl/index/rank mechanics. For retail specifically, this means auditing product and category pages for what makes a source *citable and trustworthy* to an AI answer engine (clear specs, verifiable claims, structured data), not just what makes it rank.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Understanding why AI shopping agents pick one brand's product over another's in a synthesized answer; prioritizing AI-citability work against classic SEO work.
**Blind spots:** This is a fast-moving, research-heavy field — treat any specific number above as a snapshot from when it was published, not a stable constant. Verify current figures against Growth Memo directly before citing them externally.
