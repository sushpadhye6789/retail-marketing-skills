# Taylor Holiday

**Lens:** Top-line revenue and platform-reported ROAS are the wrong numbers to run an ecommerce business on — contribution margin (gross profit minus all variable marketing and fulfillment costs) is the number that actually determines whether growth is making the business more or less valuable.

## Public framework

Holiday (CEO of Common Thread Collective, a DTC/ecommerce-focused agency) has built a consistent, widely-repeated public argument: "let go of the top-line revenue goal" and reorient the entire business around contribution margin instead — set a contribution margin goal, track it daily, and use it (not platform ROAS) as the number that decides whether to scale spend. CTC's internal system ("Prophit Engineer," per his public commentary) diagnoses performance problems by tracing backward from contribution margin down to the specific campaign or channel decision causing the drag, rather than reacting to ROAS dashboards in isolation. His stated results working with mid-eight-figure ecommerce brands under this discipline include EBITDA margins in the 45% range.

## Retail application

This is close kin to `budget-allocation` and `budget-pacing-tracker`'s existing discipline, and a direct challenge to any retailer still making spend decisions off blended or platform-reported ROAS alone: Holiday's argument is that ROAS can look healthy while contribution margin (the number that actually funds the business) is deteriorating, because ROAS doesn't account for product cost, fulfillment, returns, and other variable costs baked into the actual unit economics.

## Best for / blind spots

**Best for:** Diagnosing whether "good ROAS" campaigns are actually contributing to business health once real variable costs are accounted for; making the case to leadership for contribution-margin-based decision-making over top-line growth targets.
**Blind spots:** Requires accurate, granular cost data (true product cost, fulfillment cost, return rate by SKU/channel) to calculate correctly — a retailer without that data infrastructure needs to build it first before contribution margin can actually replace ROAS as the operating metric, not just adopt the philosophy.
