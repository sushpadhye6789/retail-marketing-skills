# Non-Technical Team Onboarding Guide: AI-Native Retail Marketing Department

## Overview
This guide explains how your marketing team can use the retail-marketing-skills repository to run effective marketing campaigns without needing technical expertise. Think of this as your team's marketing playbook that helps you make better decisions based on real customer insights and data.

## The Simple Idea: AI CMO OS
The system works like having a smart marketing assistant that helps you:
1. **Know your customers better** (through research)
2. **Create marketing that actually works** (through proven skills)
3. **Learn from what happens** (through simple measurements)
4. **Keep improving** (by updating your strategy based on results)

## Your Daily Marketing Workflow (No Tech Skills Needed)

### Step 1: Start with Your Customer Knowledge
Before doing any marketing, check your customer strategy:
- **Where to look**: `.agents/marketing-strategy.md` (this is your customer Bible)
- **What you'll find**: 
  - Who your ideal customers are (Sections 2-3)
  - What problems they have (Sections 4-6)
  - What makes you special (Section 6)
  - What you're trying to achieve (Sections 7-8)

> 💡 **Tip**: Read this once a week to keep your customer knowledge fresh!

### Step 2: Pick the Right Marketing Tool
When you have a marketing task, choose the right "skill" (think of these as marketing tools in your toolbox):

| Marketing Need | Which Tool to Use | What It Does |
|----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Understanding customers better | `customer-research` | Learn what customers really think and feel |
| Writing marketing messages | `copywriting` | Create words that connect with customers |
| Running ads | `ads` | Create effective advertising campaigns |
| Sending emails/texts | `email` or `sms` | Create messages that get opened and acted on |
| Improving your website | `cro` | Make your website work better for customers |
| Planning content | `content-strategy` | Decide what content to create and when |
| Measuring results | `analytics` | See what's working and what's not |

### Step 3: Use the Tool (It's Simpler Than You Think)
Just tell the system what you need in plain English:

**Examples:**
- Instead of: "I need to figure out what our customers struggle with"
- Try: `customer-research "Learn what parents struggle with when buying baby clothes"`

- Instead of: "I need to write a welcome email for new customers"
- Try: `email "Create a welcome email series for new baby bunting customers"`

The system will:
1. Automatically check your customer strategy for guidance
2. Ask you only for information it doesn't already know
3. Give you marketing materials that are tailored to your specific customers

### Step 4: Check Your Work (Simple Quality Check)
Before using any marketing materials:
1. **Does it sound like your customers?** (Check if it uses words/phrases from your research)
2. **Does it solve a real problem?** (Check if it addresses something from Sections 4-6 of your strategy)
3. **Is it clear and simple?** (Would your ideal customer understand it immediately?)

### Step 5: Run Your Marketing & Learn
After running your marketing:
1. **Notice what happened**: Did more people buy? Did they engage with your content?
2. **Write down what you learned**: Use the simple learning template below
3. **Update your strategy**: If you learned something important, add it to your marketing strategy

## Simple Learning Template (Use After Any Marketing Activity)
Just answer these 3 questions:

**What did we try?** 
[Brief description of what marketing activity you did]

**What happened?**
[What you noticed - e.g., "More people clicked on the ad", "Customers said they liked the message", "Fewer people abandoned their cart"]

**What should we do next time?**
[One specific change based on what you learned]

## Weekly Team Habit (15 Minutes)
Set a weekly team meeting to:
1. **Review your customer strategy** (10 minutes)
   - What did we learn about our customers this week?
   - Do we need to update anything in Sections 2-3?

2. **Share marketing learnings** (5 minutes)
   - What marketing worked well?
   - What didn't work as expected?
   - What should we try differently next week?

## Monthly Check-In (30 Minutes)
Once a month:
1. **Look at your results**
   - Which marketing activities worked best?
   - Which customer segments responded best?
   - What was your return on investment?

2. **Update your strategy**
   - Add new customer insights to Sections 2-3
   - Adjust what you're trying to achieve in Sections 7-8 if needed
   - Refine what makes you special in Section 6 if you learned something new

## Connecting to Your Everyday Tools
The system works with tools you already use:

| Your Current Tool | How to Connect |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **Email (Gmail, Outlook)** | Use the email skill to draft campaigns, then copy/paste into your email tool |
| **Social Media (Facebook, Instagram)** | Use the ads skill to create ad copy, then paste into your ad platform |

| **Spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets)** | Use simple tracking sheets to record what marketing you did and what results you got |
| **Meetings (Zoom, Teams)** | Share your learnings screen or use the simple learning template |

## Simple Rules to Remember

### ✅ DO:
- Always check your marketing strategy before starting marketing work
- Use customer words and phrases when you can (from your research)
- Start small - test ideas with a small group before going big
- Write down what you learn after every marketing activity
- Share learnings with your team weekly

### ❌ DON'T:
- Skip checking your customer strategy (it's your compass)
- Assume you know what customers want without checking
- Run the same marketing over and over if it's not working
- Keep learnings to yourself - share them with your team
- Make marketing decisions based on opinions instead of customer feedback

## Your First Week: Simple Action Plan

**Day 1**: 
- Read `.agents/marketing-strategy.md` Sections 2-3 (Who are your customers?)
- Write down 3 things you learned or confirmed about your customers

**Day 2-3**:
- Pick ONE marketing activity you need to do this week
- Use the relevant skill to help you create it (just tell it what you need in plain English)
- Run your marketing

**Day 4-5**:
- Notice what happened with your marketing
- Fill out the simple learning template (3 questions)
- Share one learning with your team

**Day 6-7**:
- Review your marketing strategy again
- Update it with anything new you learned
- Plan your marketing for next week based on what worked

- Learn from results → You get better at helping them

This isn't about being perfect - it's about getting a little bit smarter every week about how to help your customers and grow your business.
