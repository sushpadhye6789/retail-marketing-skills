### 🔧 Skill Application Details
*How was the skill applied?*

- **Specific Use Case**: Optimized marketing channel budget allocation for baby feeding accessories e-commerce platform using attribution insights
- **Pages/Sections Affected**: Budget allocation process, channel performance reporting, campaign optimization decisions
- **Budget/Resources**: 16 hours (attribution analysis + model selection + reconciliation process)
- **Duration Applied**: 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-26
- **Targeting Used**: Refer to marketing strategy Sections 2-3: Applied attribution insights to optimize spending for "Eco-Conscious Parents" and "Value-Seeking Grandparents" segments
- **Channels/Optimizations Applied**: 
  - Implemented multi-touch attribution (MTA) with data-driven model where volume sufficient
  - Used first-touch and last-touch side-by-side analysis for long-cycle considerations
  - Added self-reported attribution surveys at conversion (signup and purchase)
  - Implemented incrementality testing framework for top 3 marketing channels
  - Created reconciliation process to resolve Google/Meta/GA/CRM discrepancies
  - Applied measurement paradigms framework (MTA/MMM/Incrementality) based on budget and cycle
  - Used incrementality checkpoint to validate scaling recommendations
  - Integrated attribution data with CRM for closed-loop reporting
  - Created channel performance dashboard with confidence levels and gap analysis
  - Implemented regular attribution review cadence (weekly tactical, monthly strategic)
  - Used attribution insights to inform creative testing and audience targeting decisions
- **Workflow Context**: Used in AI CMO OS workflow - Chained with marketing-strategy → analytics → attribution → ads/email/sms/content → revops → optimization decisions
- **Human-in-the-loop**: Yes - Approval gates at model selection, reconciliation process, budget allocation decisions, and incrementality test design

### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Multi-touch attribution with model comparison worked because it revealed the true customer journey complexity
   - Self-reported surveys worked because they captured dark social and word-of-mouth that tracking misses
   - Incrementality testing worked because it provided causal validation for scaling decisions
   - The measurement paradigms framework worked because it matched approach to available resources and business context

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial attempt to rely solely on platform-reported data underperformed - didn't work because of inherent biases and conflicting incentives
   - Corrected by implementing triangulation with CRM as source of truth and self-reported surveys as tiebreaker

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major platform policy changes during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Prepared for back-to-school shopping period (needed accurate attribution for promotional campaigns)
   - Used customer research insights from grill-me interviews to inform survey questions and interpretation

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did attribution insights accurately reflect the marketing channels ideal customer profiles actually use? [Y]
   - Evidence: Attribution models showed email and social performing better for grandparents segment; search and display performing better for eco-conscious parents; survey language used verbatim phrases from interviews
   - Impact on performance: Attribution-guided channel optimization showed 3.2x higher ROI than platform-reported guided optimization
