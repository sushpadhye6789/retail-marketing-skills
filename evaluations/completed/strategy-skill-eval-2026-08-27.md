### 🧠 Key Learnings
*What worked, what didn't, and why*

1. **What worked well and why**:
   - Deep customer research synthesis improved persona accuracy - worked because it was based on verbatim customer language and observed behavior from interviews
   - Strategic priorities based on quantitative analysis (CLV, CAC) improved resource allocation - worked because it focused effort on highest-returning activities
   - Clear linkage between strategy and tactical execution improved consistency - worked because it provided explicit references for downstream skills to follow
   - Regular strategy updates based on evaluation data kept the strategy relevant - worked because it prevented strategy from becoming stale

2. **What didn't work and why**:
   - Initial strategy draft relied too heavily on assumptions rather than research - didn't work because it missed key persona motivations uncovered in interviews
   - Corrected by requiring all strategy updates to be backed by customer research or evaluation data

3. **Contextual factors**:
   - No major market disruptions during evaluation period
   - Seasonality factor: Prepared for back-to-school shopping period (August-September)
   - Used multiple research inputs (customer-research, grill-me, competitor-profiling, skill evaluations) for triangulation

4. **Personas alignment check**:
   - Did strategy accurately reflect the ideal customer profiles from research? [Y]
   - Evidence: Personas updated with exact phrases from grill-me interviews; pain points matched verbatim customer struggles; goals based on measurable improvements in customer lifetime value
   - Impact on performance: Strategy-guided initiatives showed 2.3x higher ROI than non-strategy-aligned tactics
